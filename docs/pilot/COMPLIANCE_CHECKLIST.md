# Pilot Terms and Compliance Checklist

- [ ] Pilot tenant accepted terms, privacy notice, and data-processing scope.
- [ ] Commerce platform app scopes are read-only and least-privilege.
- [ ] Trend and supplier sources permit the configured collection method.
- [ ] Restricted-category denylist and manual-review categories are configured.
- [ ] Marketing claims requiring evidence are reviewed before publication.
- [ ] Human approval is required for every external mutation.
- [ ] Secret rotation owner and incident contact are recorded.
- [ ] Data retention and tenant deletion procedures are acknowledged.
- [ ] Backup restore drill and tenant-isolation test passed before onboarding.
- [ ] AI limitations and recommendation-not-guarantee language are visible to operators.
